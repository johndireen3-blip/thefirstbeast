Why this site

Revelation 13 is one of the most quoted and most misused chapters of the Bible. This site sets out what has actually been revealed about its beasts, mark by mark, from Scripture, the Church's teaching, the Fathers and Doctors, and the saints. Every claim carries its level of authority. The site names no one, sets no dates, and does not weigh anyone's theory. It gives the ground on which any reading can be tested.

Its aim is knowledge and vigilance in love (Revelation 2:4), and its last word is always the Lamb's victory (Revelation 17:14).

The image in the logo

The logo is taken from this fresco. The figure who looks like Christ is the Antichrist; the devil whispers into his ear the words he will preach. Signorelli painted the counterfeit the way Scripture describes it: a likeness of Christ, with Satan's voice behind it. The same detail was used on the cover of Fr. Vincent Miceli's The Antichrist (1981).

The first marks

The site is being built a mark at a time. These are ready to read, as drafts:

  • Mark 1: Power on loan from the dragon (Revelation 13:2, 4)
  • Mark 2: Built from Daniel's beasts (Revelation 13:1–2; Daniel 7)
  • Mark 3: A counterfeit resurrection (Revelation 13:3, 12, 14)
  • Mark 5: Boasting and blasphemy (Revelation 13:1, 5–6; Daniel 7)
  • Mark 6: Worship demanded (Revelation 13:4, 8, 12, 15; Daniel 3)
  • Mark 7: War on the holy ones (Revelation 13:7, 10, 15; Daniel 7)
  • Mark 8: Universal reach (Revelation 13:7–8; 17:12–18)
  • Mark 12: A limited time (Revelation 13:5; Daniel 7:25)
  • Mark 13: A number that calls for wisdom (Revelation 13:18)
  • Mark 14: The response asked of the faithful (Revelation 13:9–10; 14:12)
  • Mark 4: A counterfeit lamb (Revelation 13:11–12), the first of the marks of the beast from the earth
  • Mark 9: Deception by signs (Revelation 13:13–14)
  • Mark 10: An image that speaks (Revelation 13:14–15)
  • Mark 11: Control of buying and selling (Revelation 13:16–17)

And one of the figures: the harlot and the scarlet beast (Revelation 17–18).

How to read what you find here

Each claim is labeled: Scripture Church teaching Fathers in agreement A Father or Doctor A theologian Approved apparition (aid) Disputed. Approved apparitions are aids to faith, never additions to it (CCC 67). Where interpreters differ, each position is set out as its holders state it, and the Church's guidance is given; the site does not decide what the Church has left open.

Do not be afraid

The beast's time is measured (Revelation 13:5), Christ's victory is already won (CCC 2853), and the faithful are sealed (Revelation 7:3). Revelation 13 is written so that the holy ones may endure in faith, not so that anyone may live in fear.
