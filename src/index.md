## Why this study

The twelfth chapter of Revelation stands at the center of the book. Before it, John sees the ark of the covenant in heaven. In it, a Woman gives birth, a dragon makes war, and the Woman is carried into the wilderness. After it, the dragon's servants rise, the Lamb stands on Mount Zion, and at the last the dragon is destroyed and a Bride comes down from heaven.

This site follows that one story through the chapters where it is told. It is written for readers who already know the outline of Revelation and want to go further: into the Old Testament behind each image, the Greek where it matters, the Fathers and later commentators, the liturgy, and the teaching of the Church. Every claim carries its level of authority.

It names no person as any figure of the Apocalypse, and it sets no dates. Its first rule is love (Revelation 2:4), and its last word is the Lamb's victory.

## The four parts

1. **The approach** (Revelation 11:1–18): the measured temple, the two witnesses, the seventh trumpet.
2. **The center** (11:19–14:20): the ark and the Woman, the dragon, the war in heaven, the wilderness, and the Lamb on Mount Zion. Chapter 13 is studied on the partner site, [The First Beast](https://thefirstbeast.com/).
3. **The seal and the mark** (chapters 7 and 14): God's seal on the forehead, set beside the mark of the beast.
4. **The end of the story** (19:11–22:5): the dragon bound and destroyed, and the Bride, the wife of the Lamb.

The center is being written first. See [the study](/the-study) for every page planned and [how to use this study](/how-to-use-this-study) for the way each page is laid out.
