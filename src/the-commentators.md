## Still to be consulted

These commentaries matter most for Revelation 12, and they are not yet quoted here because a complete, reliable text has not yet been read for this study. Pages will be filled in from them as they are obtained.

- **St. Epiphanius of Salamis** (d. 403), who asks whether the Woman's flight may be said of Mary.
- **Quodvultdeus of Carthage** (d. about 450), on the Woman as Mary and as the Church.
- **Oecumenius** (sixth century), the earliest Greek commentary, which reads the Woman as the Mother of God.
- **St. Andrew of Caesarea** (sixth to seventh century), the standard commentary of the Greek Church.
- **St. Bede the Venerable** (d. 735), *Explanation of the Apocalypse*.
