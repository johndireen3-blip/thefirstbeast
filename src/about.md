## What this site is

The First Beast sets out what Scripture and the Church have revealed about the beasts of Revelation and Daniel, so readers can know them and keep watch without guessing who they are. It covers Revelation 13 verse by verse, and then each figure around it in depth: the dragon, the beast from the sea, the beast from the earth (the false prophet), the scarlet beast of Revelation 17, the three unclean spirits like frogs, the man of lawlessness of 2 Thessalonians, and the beasts and little horn of Daniel.

Its sources are Scripture, the Catechism and other teaching of the Church, the Fathers and Doctors, Church-approved apparitions (as aids, never additions to the faith), and the saints. Every claim is labeled by its level of authority.

## What it is not

It is not speculative. It names no person, living or dead, as any of these figures, and it sets no dates. Where a first fulfillment is part of the text's meaning, as Antiochus IV is for Daniel and imperial Rome is for John's first readers, the site says so, because the text cannot be understood without it. Other sites in The Ephesus Challenge weigh particular proposals about our own time; this one does not.

## Fr. John

Fr. John is a Catholic diocesan priest. He founded and directs The Ephesus Challenge, a family of websites on the Book of Revelation and Christian hope. This site is researched and written with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, under his direction, and he reviews its pages. He is not an academic scholar of Scripture.
