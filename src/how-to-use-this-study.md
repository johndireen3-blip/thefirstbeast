## The shape of each page

Every study page takes one verse or short passage and follows the same order:

1. **The text**, in the New American Bible, Revised Edition.
2. **The words**, where the Greek adds something a translation cannot show.
3. **The Old Testament behind it.** Revelation almost never quotes; it weaves. Most of its images come from the Law, the Prophets and the Psalms.
4. **How the Church reads it**: the liturgy, the Catechism and the popes.
5. **The Fathers and later commentators.**
6. **Where readers differ.** Honest disagreements are stated, with their sources.
7. **What it asks of us.**

## The labels

Each claim is marked with the weight it carries:

- [[auth:scripture]] The inspired text itself.
- [[auth:church]] The Catechism, the liturgy, councils and popes.
- [[auth:fathers]] A reading the Fathers hold in common.
- [[auth:father]] One Father or Doctor of the Church.
- [[auth:theologian]] A theologian or Scripture scholar.
- [[auth:apparition]] A Church-approved apparition, received as a help and never as an addition to the faith (CCC 67).
- [[auth:disputed]] A point on which faithful readers differ.

## How the Woman is read here

The Church has read the Woman of Revelation 12 in three ways that belong together: as Mary, the mother of the Messiah; as Israel, the people from whom he came; and as the Church, who bears his brothers and sisters. This study reads her first as Mary, as the liturgy does, and sets Israel and the Church beside her on every page. Pope St. John Paul II joined the readings in one sentence: by Mary's "ecclesial identification as the 'woman clothed with the sun,'" it can be said that in her "the Church has already reached that perfection whereby she exists without spot or wrinkle" (*Redemptoris Mater* 47). [[auth:church]]

## What this study does not do

It names no person, living or dead, as the dragon's servant, a beast, a false prophet or a witness, and it computes no dates (CCC 673). Where the tradition leaves a question open, a page says so and shows where there is room for more than one answer, without proposing anyone.
