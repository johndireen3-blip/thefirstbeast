## How the chapter is read here

- **The literal sense first.** What the text says, in its own setting, comes before any further reading (CCC 116).
- **First fulfillment, then further fulfillment.** For John's first readers the beast pointed to Rome and its emperors. The Church also reads in it the final trial before Christ's return (CCC 675–677).
- **With the Church.** Scripture is read with Tradition and the Magisterium, and every claim is labeled by its authority.
- **No dates, and no names.** No one knows the day or the hour (Matthew 24:36; CCC 673), and this site does not ask who the beast is today.

## The call at the center

In the middle of the chapter Revelation stops and speaks to the reader: "Whoever has ears ought to hear these words" (Revelation 13:9), and "Such is the faithful endurance of the holy ones" (Revelation 13:10). The whole chapter is written for that.
