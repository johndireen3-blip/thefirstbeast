The marks

The Marks of Revelation 13

Fourteen things Revelation 13 reveals: thirteen about the beasts, and one about the faithful, which is the measure of the rest.

Home › The Marks

✓ Reviewed by Fr. John How this site is made

A mark here is something Scripture or the Church says about the beasts: a trait, an act or a sign by which they can be known. Each mark page gives the texts, what they show, how the Fathers and the Church read them, where interpreters differ, and the response asked of us. Every claim carries its level of authority, so that a mark from Scripture is never confused with a later writer's opinion.

The first marks come from Revelation 13 alone, all of them from Scripture Scripture. Marks from the rest of Scripture, from the Church's teaching, and from the Fathers and later writers will follow, each labeled by its source.

Mark 1 · The beast from the sea

Power on loan from the dragon

The dragon hands the beast its power, throne and authority; worship of the beast is worship of the dragon.

Revelation 13:2, 4

 Mark 2 · The beast from the sea

Built from Daniel's beasts

Ten horns, seven heads, and the features of leopard, bear and lion: Daniel's four kingdoms gathered into one.

Revelation 13:1–2; Daniel 7:3–8

 Mark 3 · The beast from the sea

A counterfeit resurrection

A mortal wound healed, so that the whole earth marvels and follows.

Revelation 13:3, 12, 14

 Mark 4 · The beast from the earth

A counterfeit lamb

Horns like a lamb, but it speaks like the dragon.

Revelation 13:11

 Mark 5 · The beast from the sea

Boasting and blasphemy

A mouth given to arrogance and blasphemy against God, his name and his dwelling.

Revelation 13:1, 5–6

 Mark 6 · Both beasts

Worship demanded

The world is led to worship the beast; the second beast enforces it.

Revelation 13:4, 8, 12, 15

 Mark 7 · Both beasts

War on the holy ones

Permitted to make war on the saints and to kill those who refuse to worship.

Revelation 13:7, 15

 Mark 8 · The beast from the sea

Universal reach

Authority over every tribe, people, tongue and nation.

Revelation 13:7–8

 Mark 9 · The beast from the earth

Deception by signs

Great signs, even fire from heaven, used to deceive.

Revelation 13:13–14

 Mark 10 · The beast from the earth

An image that speaks

An image of the beast is made, given breath, and made to speak.

Revelation 13:14–15

 Mark 11 · The beast from the earth

Control of buying and selling

No one may buy or sell without the mark on hand or forehead.

Revelation 13:16–17

 Mark 12 · The beast from the sea

A limited time

Authority for forty-two months, a measured season.

Revelation 13:5

 Mark 13 · The beast from the sea

A number that calls for wisdom

The number of a man, 666, to be reckoned by one who has understanding.

Revelation 13:18

 Mark 14 · The faithful

The response asked of the faithful

Endurance and faith: not a mark of the beast, but the measure of every reading's fruit.

Revelation 13:9–10
How the marks are usedThis site sets out what has been revealed and names no one. It does not ask who the beast is today, and it sets no dates (CCC 673). The marks are given so that readers can know the beasts and keep watch, in hope.

The First Beast

What Scripture and the Church have revealed about the beasts of Revelation and Daniel, set out mark by mark with its texts and its authority, so readers can know them and keep watch without guessing who they are.
