The Millennium in Revelation: What the Thousand Years Mean

BOUND.
REIGNING.
COMING.

What is the millennium, from a Catholic perspective? Revelation 20 speaks of a thousand years. The Church reads them as the age we live in now.

SYMBOLIC ARTIST’S INTERPRETATION

01 / INTRODUCTION

What Revelation 20 says.

In Revelation 20:1–6 an angel binds Satan for a thousand years, and those who refused the mark of the beast ‘came to life and reigned with Christ for a thousand years.’ This is ‘the first resurrection.’ Afterwards Satan is released for a short time, is defeated, and the dead are judged before the great white throne. No passage of Revelation has divided Christians more.

Three broad readings have developed. Premillennialism expects Christ to return first and then reign on earth for a literal thousand years. Postmillennialism expects a golden age of the Gospel before his return. Amillennialism, the reading of St. Augustine and of the Catholic Church, holds that the thousand years are symbolic: they are the present age of the Church, between Christ’s resurrection and his return in glory.

The USCCB notes on Revelation 20 put it plainly: like the other numbers in the book, the thousand years are not to be taken literally. They stand for the long period between the binding of Satan, a symbol of Christ’s victory over death and evil, and the end of the world. During this time God’s people share in his reign through their baptismal victory over sin and death.

02 / THE SYMBOLS

Reading the thousand years.

ALREADY, NOT YET
01

REVELATION 20:1–3

SATAN BOUND

Jesus said he had tied up ‘the strong man’ to plunder his house (Mark 3:27) and saw Satan fall like lightning (Luke 10:18). The binding began with the Cross and the Resurrection. The enemy still prowls (1 Pet 5:8), but he cannot stop the Gospel from reaching the nations.

Chapter 20
02

REVELATION 20:2–7

A THOUSAND YEARS

In Revelation, a thousand means immensity, not a count. The beast is given forty-two months; Christ’s reign lasts a thousand years. Evil’s time is short; Christ’s is vast.

Numbers in Revelation
03

REVELATION 20:5–6

THE FIRST RESURRECTION

Paul tells the baptized that they have already been raised with Christ (Col 3:1; Eph 2:6). The first resurrection is the new life of grace that begins in Baptism; the second is the resurrection of the body on the last day (John 5:25–29).

Read Romans 6
04

REVELATION 20:4, 6

REIGNING WITH CHRIST NOW

The martyrs on their thrones are not waiting for the kingdom; they share it. The Second Vatican Council calls the Church ‘the kingdom of Christ now present in mystery,’ its seed and beginning on earth (Lumen Gentium 3, 5; CCC 669).

Read Chapter 5
05

REVELATION 20:7–10

SATAN RELEASED

Before the end comes a final trial, when evil is unleashed one last time (CCC 675–677). Revelation shows it ending at once: fire from heaven, and the Devil thrown into the lake of fire. The outcome was never in doubt.

Chapter 20
06

CCC 676

WHAT THE CHURCH REJECTS

The Catechism rejects ‘even modified forms’ of millenarianism, the idea of a messianic kingdom fulfilled within history. In 1944 the Holy Office ruled that the view of Christ reigning visibly on earth with the saints before the final judgment cannot be safely taught.

Read the Catechism
07

CITY OF GOD, BOOK 20

ST. AUGUSTINE

Augustine once held a literal millennium, then changed his mind. He taught that ‘the Church even now is the kingdom of Christ’ and that the thousand years stand for the whole time of the Church. His reading shaped the Latin West for fifteen centuries.

Further Reading
READ WITH CARE

Many sincere Christians, especially in evangelical churches, hold a premillennial view, often joined to belief in a rapture and a seven-year tribulation. Catholics should speak of these views with respect: they arise from taking Scripture seriously. But the Church does not share them. She reads the thousand years with St. Augustine and the USCCB notes as a symbol of the present reign of Christ, and awaits one return of Christ in glory, followed by the resurrection of the dead and the last judgment.
