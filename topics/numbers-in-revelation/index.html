In Revelation, numbers are not for calculating dates. They are a language of meaning.

SYMBOLIC ARTIST’S INTERPRETATION

01 / INTRODUCTION

How numbers work in Revelation.

The USCCB introduction to Revelation explains that the book uses symbolic numbers: ‘four signifies the world, six imperfection, seven totality or perfection,’ ‘twelve Israel’s tribes or the apostles, one thousand immensity.’ John’s first readers, steeped in the Old Testament, heard these numbers as we hear colors or musical keys: they carry meaning, not measurements.

That is why Revelation can say that John heard the number of the sealed, 144,000, and then saw a multitude no one could count (7:4, 9). The number tells us what kind of people they are, the complete People of God, not how many.

Reading the numbers symbolically is not a way of avoiding the text. It is how the text asks to be read. Treating them as a calendar or a code leads readers into the date-setting that Jesus warned against (Matt 24:36).

02 / THE SYMBOLS

The key numbers.

MEANING ≠ MATH
01

SEVEN

Totality, completeness, perfection. God’s work is whole.

WHERE IT APPEARSSeven churches, lampstands and stars (1–3); seven spirits (4:5); seven seals (6–8); seven trumpets (8–11); seven bowls (15–16). Seven beatitudes run through the book (1:3; 14:13; 16:15; 19:9; 20:6; 22:7; 22:14).

Chapter 1
02

FOUR

The world, all creation: the four corners and four winds of the earth.

WHERE IT APPEARSFour living creatures (4:6); four horsemen (6:1–8); four angels holding back the four winds (7:1).

Chapter 4
03

TWELVE

The People of God: Israel’s twelve tribes and the Church’s twelve apostles.

WHERE IT APPEARSTwenty-four elders, twelve plus twelve (4:4); twelve thousand from each tribe (7:5–8); the Woman’s crown of twelve stars (12:1); the new Jerusalem’s twelve gates, twelve foundations, 12,000 stadia and 144 cubits (21:12–17); the tree of life’s twelve kinds of fruit (22:2).

Chapter 21
04

THREE AND A HALF

Half of seven: a limited, broken time of trial that God has set an end to. Also 42 months or 1,260 days.

WHERE IT APPEARSThe holy city trampled and the two witnesses (11:2–3); their bodies lie three and a half days (11:9); the Woman in the desert (12:6, 14); the beast’s authority (13:5).

Forty-Two Months
05

A THOUSAND

Immensity, a great and uncountable number or span.

WHERE IT APPEARSThe countless angels around the throne, counted in the Greek by tens of thousands and thousands (5:11); the 144,000 (7:4); the thousand years (20:2–7), which the USCCB notes read as the whole age between Christ’s victory and his return.

Chapter 20
06

144,000

Twelve times twelve times a thousand: the complete People of God, the new Israel. Not a cap on the saved.

WHERE IT APPEARSThe sealed servants of God (7:4); the redeemed with the Lamb on Mount Zion (14:1).

Chapter 14
07

666

Six falls short of seven: imperfection tripled. The USCCB notes say it most likely stands for Caesar Nero.

WHERE IT APPEARSThe number of the beast (13:18).

666
READ WITH CARE

Over the centuries, readers have turned Revelation’s numbers into calendars, treating 1,260 days as 1,260 years, or calculating 666 from the names of popes, emperors and modern leaders, and then setting dates for the end. Every such prediction has failed. The Church reads these numbers as symbols of meaning, as the USCCB notes do, and follows Jesus’ word that ‘of that day and hour no one knows’ (Matt 24:36).
