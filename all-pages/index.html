The twenty-two chapters

  • Revelation 1 Explained: The Son of Man Among the Lampstands

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 1: John on Patmos, the Lord’s Day vision of the risen Christ, and the seven lampstands and seven stars.

  • Revelation 2 Explained: Letters to the Four Churches

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 2: Christ’s letters to four churches, with their praise, warnings and promises to those who are victorious.

  • Revelation 3 Explained: Sardis, Philadelphia and Laodicea

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 3: Christ’s letters to Sardis, Philadelphia and lukewarm Laodicea, and his promise to stand at the door and knock.

  • Revelation 4 Explained: The Throne Room of Heaven

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 4: John’s vision of God’s throne, the twenty-four elders, the four living creatures and the endless song “Holy, holy, holy.”

  • Revelation 5 Explained: The Lamb and the Sealed Scroll

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 5: the sealed scroll, the Lion of Judah who appears as a slain Lamb, and the hymn of all creation, “Worthy is the Lamb.”

  • Revelation 6 Explained: The Four Horsemen and Six Seals

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 6: the four horsemen, the martyrs beneath the altar crying “How long?”, and the cosmic signs of the sixth seal.

  • Revelation 7 Explained: The 144,000 and the Great Multitude

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 7: God’s servants sealed, the meaning of the 144,000, and the countless multitude in white washed in the blood of the Lamb.

  • Revelation 8 Explained: Silence and the First Trumpets

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 8: the seventh seal, half an hour of silence, the prayers of the saints rising as incense, and the first four trumpets.

  • Revelation 9 Explained: Locusts, Abaddon and the Euphrates

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 9: the locusts from the abyss, their king Abaddon, the army from the Euphrates, and a world that still refuses to repent.

  • Revelation 10 Explained: The Angel and the Little Scroll

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 10: the mighty angel, the little scroll sweet in the mouth and bitter in the stomach, and John’s call to prophesy again.

  • Revelation 11 Explained: The Two Witnesses

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 11: the measured temple, the two witnesses who die and rise, the seventh trumpet, and the ark of the covenant seen in heaven.

  • Revelation 12 Explained: The Woman and the Dragon

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 12: the woman clothed with the sun, the dragon, Michael’s victory, and God’s protection of his people.

  • Revelation 13 Explained: The Two Beasts, the Mark and 666

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 13: the beast from the sea, the beast from the earth, the mark of the beast and 666, each a counterfeit of the Lamb.

  • Revelation 14 Explained: The Lamb on Mount Zion

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 14: the Lamb and the 144,000 on Mount Zion, the three angels’ messages, the blessed dead, and the harvest of the earth.

  • Revelation 15 Explained: The Song of Moses and the Lamb

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 15: the victors on the sea of glass, the song of Moses and the Lamb, and the seven angels who receive the bowls of God’s wrath.

  • Revelation 16 Explained: The Seven Bowls and Armageddon

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 16: the seven bowls of God’s wrath, the plagues of Egypt retold, the meaning of Armageddon, and the call to stay awake.

  • Revelation 17 Explained: The Whore of Babylon

    Who is the Whore of Babylon? A Catholic reading of Revelation 17: the harlot on the scarlet beast, the seven hills, and the Lamb who conquers.

  • Revelation 18 Explained: The Fall of Babylon

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 18: the fall of Babylon, the call to come out of her, the laments of kings and merchants, and the millstone in the sea.

  • Revelation 19 Explained: The Wedding Feast of the Lamb

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 19: heaven’s Alleluia, the wedding feast of the Lamb, and the rider on the white horse called the Word of God.

  • Revelation 20 Explained: The Millennium and Last Judgment

    What is the millennium? A Catholic reading of Revelation 20: Satan bound, the thousand-year reign, Gog and Magog, and the last judgment.

  • Revelation 21 Explained: New Heaven, New Earth, New Jerusalem

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 21: a new heaven and a new earth, every tear wiped away, and the new Jerusalem, the Bride of the Lamb.

  • Revelation 22 Explained: The River of Life

    A Catholic reading of Revelation 22: the river and tree of life, seeing God’s face, and the Bible’s final prayer: Come, Lord Jesus!

Chapter 13 in depth

  • The Beast from the Sea: Revelation 13:1–10

    Who is the first beast of Revelation 13? Its roots in Daniel 7, the Roman Empire, the healed wound, and what it means for Christians today.

  • The Beast from the Earth and the False Prophet

    Revelation 13:11–17: the lamb-like beast that speaks like a dragon, its signs and image, and why Revelation later calls it the false prophet.

  • The Mark of the Beast: A Catholic Explanation

    What is the mark of the beast in Revelation 13? A Catholic reading of the mark, the seal of God, and allegiance in daily life.

  • 666: The Number of the Beast Explained

    Why 666? Gematria, the Nero reading, the ancient 616 variant, and how the Church Fathers approached the number in Revelation 13:18.

Topics

  • Forty-Two Months in Revelation: What Does It Mean?

    The 42 months, 1,260 days, and ‘time, times and half a time’ in Daniel and Revelation: a limited season of trial, not a countdown.

  • Numbers in Revelation: What 7, 12, 144,000 and 666 Mean

    A Catholic guide to the symbolic numbers in Revelation: seven, twelve, four, three and a half, a thousand, 144,000 and 666.

  • Revelation and the Mass: The Heavenly Liturgy

    Why Catholics hear the Mass in Revelation: the Lord’s Day, the altar, incense, the Sanctus, the Lamb of God, the wedding supper and Maranatha.

  • The Millennium in Revelation: What the Thousand Years Mean

    What does the thousand-year reign of Revelation 20 mean? A Catholic explanation of the millennium and why the Church rejects millenarianism.

  • Babylon in Revelation: Who Is the Whore of Babylon?

    Who is Babylon the great, the harlot of Revelation 17–18? A Catholic explanation of the symbol, why it meant Rome, and what it means for Christians today.

  • Armageddon in the Bible: What It Means in Revelation 16

    What is Armageddon? A Catholic explanation of Revelation 16:16, the history of Megiddo, and why the final battle is a symbol, not a prediction.

  • Is the Rapture in Revelation? A Catholic Answer

    Is the rapture in the Bible? A Catholic look at 1 Thessalonians 4:17 and Revelation, and why the Church awaits one visible return of Christ.

  • Who Are the 144,000 in Revelation? A Catholic Answer

    The 144,000 of Revelation 7 and 14 are the whole people of God, counted as his complete Israel. The number is a symbol of fullness, not a limit.

  • Who Are the Two Witnesses in Revelation 11? A Catholic Answer

    The two witnesses of Revelation 11: what John sees, what the Fathers said about Elijah and Enoch, what the Church has left open, and how to keep watch.

  • Gog and Magog in Revelation 20: What Do They Mean?

    Gog and Magog in Revelation 20 are not a country on a map. They stand for all the nations deceived into a last attack on God’s people, which God himself ends.

  • The Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse: A Catholic Answer

    Who are the four horsemen of Revelation 6? Conquest, war, famine and death, what the Fathers said about the white rider, and what the text leaves open.

  • The Seven Churches of Revelation: What the Letters Say

    What are the seven churches of Revelation 2 and 3? A Catholic guide to the seven letters, what Christ praises and rebukes, and how to read them today.

  • The Antichrist: What the Catholic Church Teaches

    What the Catholic Church teaches about the Antichrist: a final trial and a religious deception in which man puts himself in the place of God. She names no one and sets no date.
